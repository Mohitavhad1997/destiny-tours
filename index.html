About Destiny Tours

Crafting Journeys That Become Lifelong Memories

Destiny Tours is a Bhiwandi-based holiday planning studio led by Vivek Naykodi, dedicated to designing personalised travel experiences across India and the world. From serene hill escapes to sun-soaked island getaways, every itinerary is built around comfort, value and unforgettable moments.

We handle everything — flights, stays, visas and on-ground experiences — so you can simply pack your bags and enjoy the journey.

Our Story

Built On Trust, Driven By Wanderlust

Destiny Tours began in Bhiwandi, Maharashtra, with a simple belief — that every family deserves a holiday that feels effortless and unforgettable. Under the leadership of director Vivek Naykodi, what started as personalised trip planning for friends and family has grown into a full-service travel studio trusted by hundreds of travellers across India.

Today, Destiny Tours designs journeys to over a dozen destinations — from the misty hills of Himachal to the turquoise lagoons of the Maldives — always with the same promise: honest pricing, dependable service and a personal touch on every trip.
